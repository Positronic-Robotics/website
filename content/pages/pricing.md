<section class="band" id="charges"><div class="rail"><span class="label">How we charge</span></div><div class="col"><h2>You pay for what the run used</h2><p>When you submit, we hold credits for each episode: its fee and its time limit. When the run ends, we charge each episode for its fee and the time it recorded. We release the rest.</p><p>We do not charge for a fault on our side or for a recording that is not valid.

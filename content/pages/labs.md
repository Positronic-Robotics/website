<section class="band" id="program"><div class="rail"><span class="label">What a program includes</span></div><div class="col"><h2>Checkpoint in, report out</h2><p>The lab, the operators and the resets are ours. An infrastructure problem becomes an API call.</p><ol class="pf-loop pf-program"><li><b>Set up</b><span>We set up your tasks on our robots. Same tasks, same scenes, scoring fixed up front.</span></li><li><b>Run</b><span>We run each new checkpoint, blind, against a maintained baseline or your previous checkpoint.</span></li><li><b>Report</b><span>You get a report per checkpoint: video for every run, the scores and the comparison.</span></li><li><b>Invoice</b><span>We invoice your lab under a contract.</span></li></ol><p>You send a served endpoint, or the weights. Keep them on your own servers if you prefer. Plenty of teams do.</p></div></section>

<section class="band" id="proof"><div class="rail"><span class="label">Who works with us</span></div><div class="col"><h2>Labs that run their models on our robots</h2><p class="point"><b><a href="https://runwayml.com/product/robotics">Runway</a></b> is our design partner.</p><figure class="quote"><blockquote>Positronic lets us evaluate checkpoints continuously as we train, so we can course-correct our research quickly. Day-to-day evaluation doesn’t compete with our robot fleet or operators for time, which keeps them focused on data collection and real-world deployment.</blockquote><figcaption>Andy Chen, Head of Robotics, Runway</figcaption></figure><p class="point"><b>Black Forest Labs.</b> FLUX 3 Action is ranked on <a href="https://phail.ai">PhAIL</a>, our public leaderboard, on a real arm and in two simulators.</p></div></section>

<section class="band"><div class="rail"><span class="label">Independence</span></div><div class="col"><h2>We train no models and we sell no robots</h2><p>Nothing of ours is on the leaderboard, so no result of ours is a result about us. A private eval runs the same way: the same rigs, the same tasks and the same scoring as the public board, on your checkpoint, and the numbers go to you alone.</p><p>The rigs are in the EU (Cyprus), with an operator who resets the scene after every attempt. Every run is recorded, and the scoring is fixed before it runs: the method is in the <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29710">PhAIL paper</a>, the harness is <a href="https://github.com/Positronic-Robotics/positronic">on GitHub</a>. On the leaderboard those recordings are public. On your eval they are yours.</p></div></section>

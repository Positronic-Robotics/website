<section class="page-head"><h1>Two engineers who measure robots</h1><p class="hero-lede">A lab will hand us the model it has spent months training and ask us what it is worth. This page is who is answering.</p></section>

<section class="band"><div class="rail"><span class="label">Why we exist</span></div><div class="col"><h2>Physical AI has no scoreboard</h2><p>A language model is graded within a day of release, in public, on numbers everyone argues about. A robot policy is graded by the team that trained it, on its own rig, against its own last checkpoint. Two labs cannot compare their work, a buyer cannot compare their options, and the field cannot tell a real advance from a good demo.</p><p>We spent our careers measuring things: ten years of search ranking at Google, where nothing ships without a metric someone can attack. Robotics has the same need and none of the apparatus.</p></div></section>

<section class="band"><div class="rail"><span class="label">Independence</span></div><div class="col"><h2>We train no models and we sell no robots</h2><p class="point"><b>We have nothing on the leaderboard.</b> We build no policy of our own, so no result of ours is ever a result about us.</p><p class="point"><b>Every run is recorded and the recordings are public.</b> Video, robot state, the score and how it was computed. Disagree with a number and you can open the episode it came from.</p><p class="point"><b>The method is published before the results are.</b> Scoring is fixed in advance, the protocol is in the <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29710">PhAIL paper</a>, and the harness that runs it is <a href="https://github.com/Positronic-Robotics/positronic">open source</a>.</p></div></section>

<section class="band"><div class="rail"><span class="label">The lab</span></div><div class="col"><h2>Real robots, in a room, with people in it</h2><p>The rigs are in Cyprus: Franka arms with DROID grippers, cameras, totes, and an operator on shift who resets the scene after every attempt and scores what she saw. Simulators run beside them on the same harness, so a policy meets LIBERO, RoboLab and a real tote through one interface.</p><p>That room is the product. It is also why a customer does not need one.</p></div></section>

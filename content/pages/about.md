<section class="band"><div class="rail"><span class="label">Independence</span></div><div class="col"><h2>We train no models and we sell no robots</h2><p>Nothing of ours is on the leaderboard, so no result of ours is a result about us.</p><p>The rigs are in Cyprus, with an operator who resets the scene after every attempt. Every run is recorded and public, and the scoring is fixed before it runs: the method is in the <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29710">PhAIL paper</a>, the harness is <a href="https://github.com/Positronic-Robotics/positronic">on GitHub</a>.</p></div></section>

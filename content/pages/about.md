<section class="page-head"><h1>The best thing we can do for Physical AI is measure it properly</h1><p class="hero-lede">We met at Google, and we both worked on Search there. Physical AI does not have one yet, so we are building it.</p></section>

<section class="band"><div class="rail"><span class="label">Independence</span></div><div class="col"><h2>We train no models and we sell no robots</h2><p>Nothing of ours is on the leaderboard, so no result of ours is a result about us. A private eval runs the same way: the same rigs, the same tasks and the same scoring as the public board, on your checkpoint, and the numbers go to you alone.</p><p>The rigs are in the EU (Cyprus), with an operator who resets the scene after every attempt. Every run is recorded, and the scoring is fixed before it runs: the method is in the <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29710">PhAIL paper</a>, the harness is <a href="https://github.com/Positronic-Robotics/positronic">on GitHub</a>. On the leaderboard those recordings are public. On your eval they are yours.</p></div></section>

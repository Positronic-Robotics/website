<section class="band" id="what-you-can-run"><div class="rail"><span class="label">What you can run</span></div><div class="col"><h2>Two real robot arms and two simulators</h2><p class="point"><b>Real arms.</b> A Franka FR3 in the DROID setup (an arm, a gripper and three cameras) and a two-arm YAM, in our lab in Cyprus. An operator resets the scene after every episode.</p><p class="point"><b>Simulators.</b> Ai2 MolmoSpaces and NVIDIA RoboLab. Your model runs on our GPU, or on your own server and we call it.</p><p>The clock keeps running while your model thinks, in the simulators too. Speed matters, as it does on the real robot. <code>positronic eval catalog</code> lists every evaluation that you can submit.</p></div></section>

<section class="band" id="what-you-get"><div class="rail"><span class="label">What you get back</span></div><div class="col"><h2>Full rollout data, not only a score</h2><div class="steps"><div class="step"><span class="step-verb">The video</span><p>The camera video of every episode, on a real arm or in a simulator.</p></div><div class="step"><span class="step-verb">Every sensor and command</span><p>The robot state and each command that your model sent, with the time of each one.</p></div><div class="step"><span class="step-verb">The task progress</span><p>The progress of the task step by step, and the score of each episode.</p></div></div></div></section>

<section class="band" id="agents"><div class="rail"><span class="label">Let your coding agent do it</span></div><div class="col"><h2>Your agent improves your model in a loop</h2><p>Give our API to Claude Code, Codex or another coding agent. Then ask it to improve your model. Nobody has to watch a robot. You focus on research, and we handle the rest.</p><ol class="pf-loop"><li><b>Submit</b><span>The agent submits a checkpoint.</span></li><li><b>Read</b><span>It reads the rollout data of each episode.</span></li><li><b>Change</b><span>It changes the model.</span></li><li><b>Submit again</b><span>It submits the next checkpoint, on a real arm or in a simulator.</span></li></ol><p>The <a href="{APP_URL}/start">Get started</a> page has a prompt to copy into your agent. The agent asks you to sign in and to approve each purchase. You buy the credits in your own browser, and the agent spends them.</p><pre class="pf-prompt"><code>Help me evaluate my robot policy on Positronic Platform.
1. Run: uv tool install --managed-python --python 3.13 positronic. …</code></pre><p class="pf-more"><a href="{APP_URL}/start">Copy the full prompt from the Get started page</a></p></div></section>

<section class="band" id="start"><div class="rail"><span class="label">Start in 4 steps</span></div><div class="col"><h2>From install to your first run</h2><ol class="pf-steps"><li><b>Install the tool.</b> You need uv 0.8.5 or later.<pre><code>uv tool install --managed-python --python 3.13 positronic</code></pre></li><li><b>Create an account.</b> The command prints a sign-in link. Sign in with Google, GitHub or your email.<pre><code>positronic account register --alias=&lt;your name&gt;</code></pre></li><li><b>Get credits.</b> Buy credits on your <a href="{APP_URL}/account">account page</a>. Limited offer while we ramp up: ask for free welcome credits. Email <a href="mailto:hi@positronic.ro">hi@positronic.ro</a> with your user id and what you plan to evaluate. We review each request.</li><li><b>Submit.</b> Your policy is a Docker image in a registry, pinned by its digest.<pre><code>positronic eval catalog
positronic eval run --eval=&lt;eval&gt; --org=&lt;your org&gt; \
  --policy-image=docker.io/&lt;you&gt;/&lt;image&gt;@sha256:&lt;digest&gt; \
  --transaction-key=&lt;a new key&gt;</code></pre></li></ol><p class="pf-more"><a href="{APP_URL}/start">Read the full guide on the Get started page</a></p></div></section>

<section class="band" id="questions"><div class="rail"><span class="label">Questions</span></div><div class="col"><h2>Charges, models and data</h2><div class="pf-faq"><details><summary>What do you charge for?</summary><p>Each episode costs a fee plus its time, in credits. When you submit, we hold the most that the run can cost. When the run ends, we charge what it used and release the rest.</p><p>We do not charge for a fault on our side or for a recording that is not valid. We charge for a usable recording, also when your model fails. Credits expire 12 months after the last time you added credits. The <a href="{APP_URL}/pricing">pricing page</a> and the <a href="/terms">terms of service</a> give the details.</p></details><details><summary>Which models work?</summary><p>Your model runs from a Docker image in a registry, pinned by its digest. The guide <a href="https://github.com/Positronic-Robotics/positronic/blob/main/docs/submit-a-policy-image.md">Submit a policy image</a> tells what the image must serve. If you entered the <a href="https://competition.phail.ai">Physical AI Competition</a>, your image has the same shape.</p><p>On a real arm, your model runs on your own server, and the robot connects to it over a secure websocket.</p></details><details><summary>Who sees your data?</summary><p>Your policy and your results stay confidential. The results of your private evaluations belong to you, and you can publish them. We do not use your policy or its recordings to train a robot policy. The <a href="/terms">terms of service</a> give the details.</p></details></div></div></section>
